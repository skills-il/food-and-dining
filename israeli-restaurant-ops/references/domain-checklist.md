# Domain Checklist: israeli-restaurant-ops

Scope: help Israeli restaurant owners with menus, delivery-platform pricing, reviews, food cost, and daily operations. Category: food-and-dining.

## Must cover (core)
- The major Israeli delivery platforms (Wolt, 10bis/תן ביס, Mishloha/משלוחה) with current commission ranges, payment cycles, and customer-base strengths; the 10bis dual consumer/corporate model; Cibus Pluxee as the corporate-meal-benefit competitor.
- Pricing with commission + VAT (18%) awareness; the menu-price formula; packaging cost.
- Hebrew menu-copy optimization (natural Hebrew, sensory words, dietary + kosher labels).
- Review-response templates (positive / quality / delivery / wrong-item) in Hebrew.
- Food-cost % targets by restaurant type; menu-engineering matrix (Stars/Puzzles/Plowhorses/Dogs).
- Daily operations: opening/closing checklists, food-safety temperature log (cold <4C, hot >60C), Israeli-calendar scheduling (Shabbat, holidays, Pesach chametz, Yom HaZikaron->HaAtzmaut), Shabbat/holiday labor-pay note.
- Kosher levels: כשר / כשר למהדרין / כשר לפסח are distinct.

## Should cover (advanced)
- Per-platform menu structure tips; combos; insulated packaging; preparation-time settings; per-platform pricing strategy.

## Out of scope (explicit)
- Cooking recipes, personal meal planning, non-Israeli delivery platforms.

## Authoritative sources
- Platform sites: 10bis.co.il, mishloha.co.il, explore.wolt.com (merchant), cibus.co.il (Cibus Pluxee).
- gov.il National Food Service (food safety / licensing); VAT 18% (Israel Tax Authority).
