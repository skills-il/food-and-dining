# Changelog

## 1.4.0 - 2026-08-24

Rewrote the kashrut section for Amendment No. 5 to the Prohibition of Kashrut Fraud Law (Sefer HaChukim 3548, 19.07.2026), which repealed the 2021 Kahana reform: only the Rabbanut may grant kashrut, a city rabbi may not certify outside his city without Chief Rabbinate Council approval, and the private-corporation route is closed (it was largely never implemented before repeal). The supervisor-payment provisions commence 19.07.2028 and are stated as a future change, with the grandfather clause for existing employment. Removed Tzohar as a certification route from the description and reference links.

Added the licensing layer the skill was missing: resolving the licensing item before anything else, the shortened licensing routes, the uniform specification, the fee (381 NIS per item from 01.04.2026, 190.5 NIS for a replacement, re-indexed twice a year), and that validity is set per item and runs one to fifteen years rather than annually. Added that planning precedes licensing and that a licence does not transfer on sale. Named the operative sanitary regulations for eating places and split the two food-import routes.

Added broad bean (pol) as Israel's fifteenth allergen under Appendix 3, which has no European counterpart and is marked differently.

Removed two fabrications: a non-existent 2024 "Cooked Food Delivery Regulations", and a claimed 2024 revision of the red-label thresholds (they have two stages, 2020 and 2021, and have not moved since). Corrected per-serving nutrition and the barcode from mandatory legal fields to optional and commercial respectively, and corrected an inverted minimum-font-size rule in the labelling reference. Replaced two dead gov.il URLs and flagged the unsourced practitioner figures in the health-ministry reference as orientation rather than citable requirements.

## 1.3.3 - 2026-08-11

Updated the red-label thresholds to the second-stage values in force since January 2021 (sodium 400/300 mg, sugars 10/5 g, saturated fat 4/3 g). The skill carried the first-stage January 2020 values while labelling them 'as of 2024', and its reformulation example (510 to 490 mg sodium) would not have cleared the real threshold. Added the EU Regulation 1169/2011 transition: the current allergen format holds until 01.01.2028 for pre-packed food and 01.01.2032 for bulk, after which allergens are marked only inside the ingredients list.

## 1.3.2 - 2026-08-11

Removed the fabricated "up to 226,000 NIS" licensing penalty. Replaced two dead Ministry of Health URLs (one redirects to a 404, one returns HTTP 200 with an error body) with the live food-labeling page.
