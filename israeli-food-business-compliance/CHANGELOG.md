# Changelog

## 1.3.3 - 2026-08-11

Updated the red-label thresholds to the second-stage values in force since January 2021 (sodium 400/300 mg, sugars 10/5 g, saturated fat 4/3 g). The skill carried the first-stage January 2020 values while labelling them 'as of 2024', and its reformulation example (510 to 490 mg sodium) would not have cleared the real threshold. Added the EU Regulation 1169/2011 transition: the current allergen format holds until 01.01.2028 for pre-packed food and 01.01.2032 for bulk, after which allergens are marked only inside the ingredients list.

## 1.3.2 - 2026-08-11

Removed the fabricated "up to 226,000 NIS" licensing penalty. Replaced two dead Ministry of Health URLs (one redirects to a 404, one returns HTTP 200 with an error body) with the live food-labeling page.
